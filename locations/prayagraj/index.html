Prayagraj FAQs

What does a custom LMS cost?

LMS builds are custom-quoted — recorded courses plus tests plus fee tracking is the lean starting point most institutes need first.

Can you migrate our existing YouTube/WhatsApp setup?

Yes — we structure your existing content into the platform and give students one place for everything.

Are law firm websites allowed to advertise?

The Bar Council restricts solicitation. We build within the rules: informative, educational, authority-building — never ambulance-chasing.

How long does an LMS take to build?

A lean first version typically takes 5–8 weeks depending on courses, tests and payment integration.
