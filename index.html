Straight answers

Before you message us.

Quick answers to the questions most clients ask.

Who is Compass Digital?
Compass Digital is a website design and development studio in Lucknow. We build custom business websites and e-commerce stores for clients across India.
What kind of websites do you build?
We build business websites that bring enquiries and e-commerce stores for online sales. We plan the pages around your business and what customers need to do.
Who do you build websites for?
We work with businesses of any size and in any industry. Common examples include clinics, hotels, manufacturers, retailers, architects and local services.
Will you use a template?
No. We may reuse reliable code, but the design, pages and user flow are planned for your business.
Can you improve my business website instead of rebuilding it?
Yes, if the current website has a solid base. We may improve key pages instead of rebuilding everything.
Do I need a website if I already get customers from Instagram/WhatsApp?
A website gives customers one clear place to understand your offer, see proof and take the next step. Instagram and WhatsApp can still bring attention and conversations.
Should I get a business website or a full online store?
Choose a business website for enquiries, appointments or calls. Choose an online store when customers need to browse products, pay online and place orders.
How much does a business website cost in India?
A business website starts from ₹15,000. The final cost depends on the pages, features, content and integrations you need.
What is the cost of an ecommerce website in India?
An e-commerce website costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000. The final cost depends on the catalogue, payments, shipping, integrations and custom features.
Website banane me kitna kharcha aata hai?
Business website ₹15,000 se start hoti hai. E-commerce website ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 ki range mein hoti hai. Final cost pages, features aur integrations par depend karti hai.
How long does it take to build a business website?
A simple business website can go live in 7 days. Most standard builds take 2 to 3 weeks. Custom or e-commerce stores take 3 to 4 weeks.
What should I share if I already have a website?
Share your current website, or a site you like. Tell us about your business and what you want the website to do.
What happens after the free strategy session?
You keep the recommendations. If we are a good fit, we share a paid plan. There is no obligation to continue.
Do you provide ongoing updates?
Yes. We can update products, catalogues and content when needed. The price depends on the amount and frequency of work.
Do you also maintain the website after it is built?
Yes. Care plans can cover technical upkeep and regular website changes after launch.
What should my website do to bring me more customers?
It should state your offer clearly, show proof, load quickly on mobile and make the next step obvious.
How do I get more customers from my website?
Bring the right visitors to the site and give them a clear reason to act. The website should make it easy to call, book, message or buy.
Website designer near me: do you work with clients outside Lucknow?
Yes. We are based in Lucknow and work with clients across India through online calls, reviews and project updates.
Who builds the best websites for small businesses in India?
Look for a partner who understands your buyers, explains the price clearly and builds for mobile. Compass Digital handles the strategy, design and development in one place.
