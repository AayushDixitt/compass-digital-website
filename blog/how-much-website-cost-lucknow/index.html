How Much Does a Website Cost in Lucknow?

Real numbers, no guesswork. What a business website actually costs in 2026 — and what makes the price go up or down.

If you run a business in Lucknow and you have asked five developers for a quote, you have probably received five completely different numbers. One says ₹8,000. Another says ₹80,000. Both claim to build you a “website”. Here is an honest breakdown of what things actually cost, and why.

The short answer

In 2026, a proper business website in Lucknow costs between ₹15,000 and ₹40,000. A full e-commerce store with payments, products and order management runs ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000. Anything far below that range is cutting something you will miss later. Anything far above it should come with a very good explanation.

At Compass Digital, our business websites start at ₹15,000 and our e-commerce builds start at ₹50,000. We will tell you exactly what that includes before you pay a rupee.

What makes the price go up

  • Number of pages. A five-page site costs less than a twenty-page site. Simple.
  • Custom design vs template. A template is cheap and looks like everyone else. A custom design costs more and looks like you.
  • Content. If you give us ready text and photos, it is faster. If we have to write everything from scratch, it takes longer.
  • E-commerce features. Product catalogues, filters, coupons, payment gateways and shipping logic each add real work.
  • Speed and SEO setup. A site that loads in one second and ranks on Google needs more care than a site that just exists.

What keeps the price honest

The cheapest way to keep costs down is to arrive prepared. Know what pages you need. Have your text and photos ready. Have one person who takes decisions. Projects go over budget when requirements change every week, not because developers are greedy.

Red flags

  • A “complete website” for ₹3,000. That is a template installed in an afternoon, with no support after you pay.
  • No written list of what is included. If it is not written, it does not exist.
  • Full payment upfront. A normal structure is part advance, part on launch.

What to ask before you pay

Ask these four questions: What exactly is included? How long will it take? What happens if I need changes after launch? Who owns the domain and the files?

If a developer answers all four clearly, in writing, you are in safe hands — whether you build with us or with someone else.
