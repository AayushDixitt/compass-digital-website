B2B E-commerce Web Design for Kanpur Exporters: Scaling Globally

International buyers check you online before they ever fly down. Here's what a Kanpur exporter's website must do to win global orders.

Kanpur's leather, saddlery and textile exporters have sold to the world for decades. But the way global buyers choose suppliers has changed. Before a buyer from Europe or the US books a flight, they do something simple: they google you. What they find in those ten minutes decides whether you get the email or your competitor does.

What global buyers actually look for

  • Real factory evidence. Photos and videos of your actual unit, your machinery, your team at work. Stock photos are spotted instantly and destroy trust.
  • Capacity and lead times. Buyers need to know: how many units per month, and how fast. Vague claims like “large capacity” mean nothing. Numbers mean everything.
  • Certifications. ISO, SEDEX, LWG for leather — whatever applies to your trade, show it with the certificate, not just the logo.
  • Clear product ranges with MOQs. Minimum order quantities upfront save both sides weeks of back-and-forth.

Catalogue first, checkout later

Most B2B exporters do not need a shopping cart. International orders are negotiated, sampled and contracted — they are not impulse purchases. What you need is a quote-based catalogue: beautiful product pages with an easy “request quote” flow, not a checkout button nobody will press.

Trust signals that close deals

List the markets you actually serve. Name the trade fairs you actually attend. Show the production process honestly. Global buyers have been burned by glossy websites with nothing behind them — proof beats polish.

The basics most exporter sites miss

An English version written properly (not machine-translated). A contact page with your full address, phone with country code, and a named person — not just a form. Load speed under three seconds, because your buyer might be browsing on hotel wifi. These sound small. Together, they are the difference between looking like a trading agent and looking like the manufacturer you are.
